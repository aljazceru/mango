<objective>
Replace the inline Providers and Defaults sections in the iOS and Android settings screens with tappable summary rows that navigate to dedicated sub-screens. Extract the full provider card content and defaults form into new sub-screen views.

Purpose: The Providers section is the visually heaviest section in Settings. Moving it and Defaults to sub-screens dramatically shortens the main settings scroll and follows platform-native Settings app patterns.
Output: 4 new view files (2 per platform), 4 modified files (settings + routing per platform).
</objective>
