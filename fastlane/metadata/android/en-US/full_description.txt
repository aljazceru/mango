No provider lock-in. No subscriptions. Own your data. Bring your own key. Protect your chats. And delete them if forced to unlock the app. No tracking, no analytics.

PPQ can be set up automatically inside Mango (no account on ppq.ai needed first), funded via any external Lightning wallet by scanning a QR, and backed up to an encrypted device backup (.mppq) with a password. PPQ holds the balance (prepaid USD credit); Mango never touches funds. Duress wipe preserves PPQ credentials. BYOK and custom providers remain supported.

Confidential providers run inference inside a hardware-isolated Trusted Execution Environment, attested and verified on-device (AMD SEV-SNP / Intel TDX). Local and custom (bring-your-own-key) providers are not attested — their privacy depends on the operator you choose. Conversations, documents and vector indexes stay local. Optional on-device models for fully offline chat.
